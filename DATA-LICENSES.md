# Data licences

Each layer keeps its source terms when baked. Local Bay packs and derived tiles live outside Git. The tracked SF pilot release manifests and landing metadata include credits for their inputs. Planet tiles use Overture. The page's blended tiles can contain both Overture and local lidar. Credit each contributing source when distributing the corresponding data. [Source checks and release details](.agents/skills/bay-sources/SKILL.md)

On November 25, 2026 the MapServer and both layer records had empty description and copyright fields. The DataSF catalogue returned zero results for either layer name. Its [terms](https://data.sf.gov/terms-of-use) cover downloads from DataSF. The [city policy](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Data_Management_Policy_--_COIT_October_2025.pdf) assigns a default licence to portal publications. Neither identifies these MapViewer layers. No terms were found for either layer. On November 25, 2026 the maintainer chose to ship these positions of public street infrastructure with credit to San Francisco Public Works. For a bake using only OSM for lamps and overhead paths, pass `--no-public-works-fixtures`.

The pinned 155-square release combines the USGS survey, NAIP imagery, the DataSF layers above, OSM streets and transit, Overture geometry, Mapterhorn terrain and Mapillary street observations. The city shoreline sets the survey extent. The city pack has no Mapzen extract, Meta/WRI canopy raster or separate bay-floor DEM. The vehicles are authored models. The release carries the photographers’ source-image links alongside the derived street colours and sign credits.
