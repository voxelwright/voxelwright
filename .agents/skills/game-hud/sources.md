# Game HUD sources: what was opened, and what wasn't

These verdicts come from a second pass on 2026-11-21 over game-specific claims. **Opened** means the page's text
was read. **Snippet** means only a search summary was seen. Quote a game as precedent only where the verdict is
confirmed.

| Claim | Verdict | Source and words |
|---|---|---|
| Apple: show the stick where the thumb lands and hide it until touched; keep off the Home indicator and the Dynamic Island | **Confirmed** (opened, through the page's JSON mirror) | [Game controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls): "opt to show a virtual thumbstick wherever the player lands their thumb instead of a static thumbstick position"; "consider hiding movement controls until a player touches the screen to reduce the amount of UI overlapping your game content"; "When the thumbstick is at rest, the virtual control fades to show it's not in use." |
| Call of Duty Mobile: Simple and Advanced fire modes, and a stick that can be locked in place | **Confirmed** (opened) | [Activision blog, October 2019](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-10/Getting-a-Grip-on-the-Call-of-Duty-Mobile-Controls): "switch between Simple and Advanced Fire modes"; the joystick setting "lock[s] the Joystick to one location on screen... instead of having it move when pressing the left side of the screen." So the default floats. |
| Ghost of Tsushima: no waypoint HUD, minimal by design | **Confirmed** (opened, interview with the creative director) | [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/ghost-tsushima-creative-director-interview): "how can we keep people immersed in this world instead of just more compasses and mini-maps on the screen"; "some options in the menus to... make it a more minimal experience". The map still has pins. |
| GTA V: the weapon and radio wheels slow time | Reported (a mod page that removes the effect) | GTA5-Mods, "No Radio/Weapon Wheel Slowdown": "disabling the time slowdown/ screen effects that occur when using the in-game weapon or radio wheel". No Rockstar page was opened. |
| Ratchet & Clank: Quick Select pauses (2016) or slows time (Rift Apart) | Reported (snippets) | Fandom wiki and press reviews, not opened. |
| Red Dead Redemption 2: the D-pad switches the HUD between off, minimal and default | Reported (opened, press quoting the game) | mp1st and PlayStation LifeStyle: "toggle between several different HUD options: off, a minimized version, and the default setting." The PC "contextual" settings were not confirmed. |
| PUBG Mobile: the control layout can be moved | Partly confirmed (opened, official help) | PUBG Mobile help centre: "you may change the location of the in-game control keys". Size, opacity and stick type were not confirmed. |
| Genshin Impact: Attack, Skill, Burst, Jump and Sprint keys at the bottom right, stick at the bottom left | Partly confirmed (opened, a third-party guide) | touchtapplay.com lists the keys; the arc layout is only in an image. No HoYoverse page was opened. |
| Fortnite: the HUD Layout Tool moves, resizes, adds and removes buttons | Snippet (Epic pages returned 403) | "Simply move or resize buttons with ease or take more advanced steps and add/remove buttons to the HUD." |
| Roblox: Dynamic Thumbstick appears where the thumb lands, and jump is at the bottom right | **Unverified** | The enum pages exist (DevTouchMovementMode, TouchMovementMode), but their text is blank. The DevForum announcement did not open. |
| Tears of the Kingdom: time pauses or slows in the quick menu and the ability wheel | **Unverified** | No Nintendo page was opened. Don't state it. |
| Death Stranding: the HUD shows on a touchpad press | **Unverified** | Don't state it. |

The first pass (also 2026-11-21) opened Apple's game-controls and accessibility pages, XAG 101, 102 and 107,
the Game Accessibility Guidelines list, Android's touch target page, the Minecraft wiki's Hotbar page, Wikipedia's
pie menu page (citing Callahan et al. 1988) and Hoober's 2013 UXmatters study. SKILL.md cites them inline.
