Village's observed integration needs, not certification of the whole platform.

the requested behavior. Read only the relevant reference:

owner authorization and release policy outside candidate/model control. A role
name, separate session, city or rig is not proven family-data isolation. Native
agent completion is not independent verification. Reconcile external effects
before retrying interrupted work.

Refresh affected sections when the binary, imported pack lock, provider/runtime,
formula compiler contract or store changes, or a probe contradicts this pack.

semantics; record source-only versus executed evidence. A new missing capability
requires a narrow investigation, not a conclusion that custom infrastructure is

change, with originating evidence and correction status; private facts do not
