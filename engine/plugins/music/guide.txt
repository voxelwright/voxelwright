world.music({like, pace, mode, drive, now})   says what the realm's music is like, for everyone in it. It is not a track: the page composes it as it plays and it never repeats, so say what it should be like and change that as the rule's own numbers change (calm to hunted, the last lap, the final round). Only the fields given change. like is one of: town (a deep house groove, 116 beats a minute); coast (a lilt in six, guitar and flute, 84 beats a minute); sea (no pulse: long tones and far bells, 48 beats a minute); snow (bells, and space between them, 60 beats a minute); night (slow, a few notes on a piano, 66 beats a minute); danger (a driving figure over low drums, 138 beats a minute); making (bright and busy: a marimba at work, 122 beats a minute). pace 40 to 180 is beats a minute in place of its own. mode is the scale in place of its own: major, lydian, mixolydian, dorian, minor, phrygian, pentatonic (major and lydian are bright, dorian and minor darker, phrygian threatening). drive 0 to 1 is how much of it is playing: 0 one quiet voice, 1 every voice; unsaid, a piece comes in a voice at a time by itself. pace and drive change at once, in the piece that is playing; a new like or mode comes in at the next bar, or at the next beat with now: true. like: null gives the music back to the place (which has music of its own by where people are). One realm has one music: the rule that changed it last has it, and when that rule is taken out the place's own comes back. After a restart say it again on start
