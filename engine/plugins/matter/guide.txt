world.matter({of, weighs})   sets how heavy a material is in this world, against water (1), from now on: of is "earth", "rock", "grass", "sand", "water", "asphalt", "concrete", "brick", "plaster", "wood", "glass", "steel", "leaves", "roofing" or "lamp"; weighs is 0.05 to 50 (steel is 7.8, brick 1.9, wood 0.6 unless set), or null for the engine's own. It changes how things that fall, fly and strike behave (light walls land softly, heavy balls hit hard), not where anything is. Set it in start: a world started again has the engine's own
