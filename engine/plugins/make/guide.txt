world.make({wish, for, at, hold})   asks for a thing to be made from words, for someone at a page: wish is what it is ("a short sword", "a hot dog in a paper tray"), up to 200 characters; for is their id in world.people (kind "page"): the thing is made by that person's own model on their own machine, so it is for the one who asked or is being given it; at is [x, z] or [x, y, z] within 30 m of them (left out: on the ground in front of them). It takes from a second (a thing like one made before) to a minute, is made of the world's own cells where it stands, and of the making itself nothing is heard back. hold: true puts it in their right hand as soon as it is made (a weapon, a hot dog, a torch): their figure carries it, world.people shows it under holds, the rule hears held with its id, and act says it was used (e.using). At most 6 a minute for one person.
