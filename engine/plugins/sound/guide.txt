world.sound({like, at, on, force, size, note, notes, gap, for, reach, patch})   makes a sound that everyone near hears where it is, quieter and later the further off. It needs what it is (like, or patch) and where (on, or at). on: an actor of this rule or an id of world.people: it sounds where they are. at: [x, z] or [x, y, z]. like is one of: blow (a fist or a stick landing on someone); slap (an open hand, a wet cloth); thud (a body or a sack falling); knock (knuckles on wood); clang (metal struck: an anvil, a pan, a shield); shot (a gun fired); bang (a shell, a firework, a door blown in); bell (a bell rung: a ring bell, a tower bell (size makes it bigger and lower)); chime (a small bright ring: a coin, a glass, a triangle); gong (a great plate struck soft); drum (a drum beaten); clap (two hands); cheer (a crowd roaring and clapping, about three seconds); strum (a guitar string; with notes, a chord strummed or a tune picked); key (a piano key; with notes, a chord or a tune); pipe (a flute or a whistled note, held for as long as asked); horn (a horn, a bugle, a car’s horn, held for as long as asked); whistle (a referee’s whistle, held for as long as asked); engine (a motor running, held for as long as asked; pitch is how fast it turns); whoosh (something passing fast through the air); splash (something into water); smash (glass or china breaking); click (a switch, a latch, an empty gun). force 0 to 1 is how hard (louder and brighter; 0.7 unless said). size 0.25 to 4 makes the same thing bigger and lower (a tower bell is a bell of size 3). note is its pitch as a MIDI note, 12 to 120 (60 is middle C, 69 is A), or pitch in Hz; notes is up to 8 notes sounded one after another, gap ms apart (0 to 1000): a chord strummed is notes with gap 25, a tune is notes with gap 250, and a longer tune is a call each tick. for is how many seconds a held one lasts (0.05 to 4). A sound stays where it began: it does not follow who it is on, and a held one stops when its for is up and cannot be changed while it lasts. Something that moves and keeps sounding (a motor) is a short held sound made again each tick where it then is: it steps, it does not glide. reach 2 to 300 is the metres at which it is half as loud as beside it (each like has its own). A rule makes at most 12 sounds a second. Nothing is heard by rules: a sound is for people's ears
  patch: a sound of your own in place of like, as data. Nothing is recorded: say what is struck, plucked, blown or rubbed and what it is. {how: "struck" | "plucked" | "blown" | "rubbed", hard: 0 to 1 (felt 0.1, wood 0.5, steel 1: harder is brighter), rough: 0 to 1 (grit, breath), shape: "bar" | "plate" | "bell" | "skin" | "string" | "tube" | "pipe" | "hollow" | "lump" | "cup", pitch: 20 to 8000 Hz of its lowest tone, ring: 0.01 to 12 seconds it takes to die away (brick 0.06, wood 0.15, glass 0.5, a bell 5; for blown or rubbed, how long it takes to stop), damp: 0 to 1 how much sooner its high tones die (metal 0.1, wood 0.7, earth 0.9), gain: 0 to 1}, all of those always; and if wanted bend: -2 to 2 octaves it starts off its pitch (a drum +0.5, a drop of water -0.5), waver: 0 to 1 a held tone trembling, pieces: {count: 1 to 40, over: seconds, spread: 0 to 1} small ones of the same after the blow (glass breaking, a rattle), air: {from: Hz, to: Hz, for: seconds, gain: 0 to 1} a rush of noise sweeping between two pitches (a crash, a puff, a hiss). No other key. It can never be louder than the page lets anything be
  as: a sound that stays on someone and is heard moving with them: world.sound({as: "motor", on: who, like: "engine", note: 40}) once, not each tick. as is a name of your own for it (letters, digits, - or _). on is an actor of this rule, an id of world.people, or the id of a thing that lives if you have been told it (a rule cannot yet list the things there: for a car someone drives, put it on the driver). It lasts until world.sound({as: "motor", stop: true}), or until who it is on goes, or this rule does. Say it again by the same name to change it while it sounds: note or pitch, force and reach glide to the new value in a third of a second (a motor rising as it speeds up is world.sound({as: "motor", note: 40 + speed}) each tick: it does not start again). One long tone follows as it is: pipe, horn, whistle, engine, or a patch that is blown, or a string, tube, pipe or hollow that is rubbed (a siren is a horn whose note you change each tick). Anything else follows by being made again: give every, 80 to 2000 ms between one and the next (running feet are {as: "feet", on: who, like: "thud", every: 300, force: 0.4}). With as there is no at, notes, gap or for. A rule has at most 6 at a time, a realm 24; each page sounds the 6 nearest it.
