Accordion

Rows of words with a chevron, parted by grooves. What opens is on the same surface. One at a time, or as many as you like.

In use: before you deploy harbor-api (one open at a time)

What does a deploy do?

It builds the main branch, runs the tests, and swaps the new build in. The one before it is kept, so a rollback takes a few seconds.

How long is a build kept?

The last three are kept. Older ones are removed the night after the fourth is made.

A build that is live is never removed, however old it is.

Who can roll back?

Anyone on fogline who can deploy. Mina and Ada are told when it happens.

Every state: closed, open, several open, a long label

Closed

Nothing is here until the row is opened.

Open

The chevron has turned over. What opened is words on the same surface as the row.
