Shipping small things
May 24, 2026

Small work is easy to underestimate. A tiny package still needs care, a clear label, and a page that says what it is for.

The goal is not to move fast. It is to hand over something whole, that the next person can build on without asking you.

pack = Box()
pack.add(item)
pack.label(to)
ship(pack)

Finish the small thing. Then send it.
